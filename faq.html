Letters to the Editor

Questions from inquisitive guests

The Editor Regrets Nothing

What should I wear?

— Underdressed in Windsor

The Editor replies: The dress code is Formal/Cocktail attire.

Where can I park?

— Circling the Block

The Editor replies: The Urban Field House has parking on site.

May I bring a plus 1?

— Hopeful in Lakeshore

The Editor replies: Guests are by invitation only; please refer to the names addressed on your invite.

Is the event indoors or outdoors?

— Checking the Forecast

The Editor replies: The reception is indoors, with an outdoor covered patio area.

Is there a gift registry?

— Well-Meaning Aunt

The Editor replies: Due to geographical challenges, we do not have a gift registry. However, a contribution to our future nest egg would be greatly appreciated.

Where can I indicate my dietary restrictions?

— Hungry on Faith Drive

The Editor replies: When filling in your RSVP or that of someone in your party, please indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies.

Please direct any further questions to us over email at YarfeL11@gmail.com.
