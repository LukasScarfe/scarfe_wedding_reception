Local Couple Already Married, Throwing Party Anyway

Dinner and dancing confirmed; your attendance requested

The pair now intend to make it everyone’s problem.

Dinner and dancing will take place Friday, May 21st, 2027, at Urban Fieldhouse, 1203 Faith Drive.

Officials urge residents to attend. Footwear suited to dancing is advised.
